Deep Cleaning

Our deep cleaning service targets the areas that are often missed in routine cleaning, leaving homes and offices feeling thoroughly refreshed.

Get a quote

What’s Included in Our Deep Cleaning Service

  • Detailed cleaning of kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas
  • Dusting, sanitizing, and polishing of high-touch surfaces
  • Deep care for floors, fixtures, and overlooked corners
  • Ideal for seasonal refreshes, post-event cleanup, or moving prep

Our Cleaning Checklists

Bedroom Checklist
  • Wipe down doors and on top of doors
  • Wipe down skirting boards and carefully dust all wooden furniture
  • Wash windows internally including sills and ledges
  • Clean inside, outside, and on top of cupboards and wardrobes
  • Remove cobwebs
  • Clean coving, picture rail, cornice, and curtain rails
  • Clean and polish mirrors and pictures
  • Clean and polish windows internally, window sills, and window ledges
  • Clean plugs, sockets, light switches, fittings, and lamp shades
  • Clean fire surround and radiators
  • Clean behind and under furniture
  • Vacuum upholstery
Kitchen Cleaning Checklist
  • Clean and polish all kitchen cupboards and drawers both inside and out
  • Clean both inside and outside refrigerator and freezer (if defrosted in advance)
  • Wipe down the microwave inside and out
  • Clean all sides of washing soap dispenser, machine, and filters
  • Clean dishwasher and remove limescale
  • Clean and degrease inside of oven and polish outside parts
  • Clean and polish hob and grill
  • Clean, degrease, and polish extractor fan and filters
  • Remove mould and grease from wall tiles, wash down, and polish
  • Clean exterior of all kitchen appliances such as kettle and toaster
  • Wipe and wash down kitchen countertops
  • Remove lime scale and polish sink and shine taps
  • Clean windows from the inside and wipe down sills and ledges
  • Wipe all woodwork including doors, frames, and skirting boards
  • Wipe down radiators
  • Clean plugs and light switches
  • Vacuum and wash hard floors
  • Clean any utensils or plates if needed
Bathroom/Toilet Checklist
  • Clean bath and remove limescale
  • Wash shower cabinet or screen from inside and out and de-scale
  • Clean and disinfect toilet from inside and out
  • Wash and de-scale sink and shine taps
  • Wash down tiles, remove mould, and wipe tiles
  • Clean and polish mirrors
  • Wipe cupboards, shelving, surfaces, and polish stainless steel
  • Clean windows from inside and wipe down sills and ledges
  • Wipe down doors, frames, and skirting boards
  • Wipe clean towel rail
  • Wipe down extractor fan
  • Clean plugs and light switches
  • Vacuum, sanitise, and mop floors
Lounge/Living Room Checklist
  • Remove cobwebs
  • Clean and polish mirrors and picture frames
  • Clean and polish windows internally, sills, and ledges
  • Wipe down doors and clean on top of doors
  • Clean skirting boards and woodwork of dust
  • Clean light switches, fittings, lamp shades, plugs, and sockets
  • Clean inside, behind, and on top of cupboards and furnishings
  • Clean behind and under furniture
  • Dust and polish all surfaces
Window Cleaning Kit Checklist
  • Clean inside and outside of windows, removing all visible and invisible dirt leaving no wipe marks
  • Clean burglar bars and remove all visible stains
  • Clean windowsills and frames
  • Clean window tracks
Facility Maintenance Checklist
  • Empty trash and recycling bins
  • Replace trash liners as needed
  • Dust desks, tables, and workstations
  • Wipe and disinfect high-touch surfaces (doorknobs, light switches, phones, keyboards, shared equipment)
  • Clean and sanitize restrooms
  • Refill soap, paper towels, and toilet paper
  • Clean mirrors and sinks
  • Wipe down kitchen countertops and tables
  • Clean microwave, coffee maker, and sink
  • Restock kitchen supplies
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Spot-clean glass doors and windows
  • Organize common areas and reception
