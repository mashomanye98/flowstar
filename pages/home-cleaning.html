Home Cleaning

From weekly maintenance to one-off visits, our home cleaning service helps keep your space comfortable, tidy, and ready for daily life.

What’s Included in Our Home Cleaning Service

  • Regular upkeep for kitchens, bedrooms, and bathrooms
  • Flexible plans for weekly, monthly, or one-off service
  • Dependable care for busy households
  • Friendly, detail-focused cleaning support

Standard Cleaning Checklists

Bedroom
  • Wipe bedside tables, dressers, and wardrobes (outside only)
  • Make beds (if required)
  • Clean mirrors
  • Empty bins
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Mop hard floors
  • Remove visible cobwebs
Kitchen
  • Wipe and sanitize countertops
  • Clean and polish sink and taps
  • Clean exterior of appliances (microwave, kettle, toaster, fridge, etc.)
  • Wipe hob/stovetop
  • Clean microwave inside and outside
  • Wipe dining table and chairs
  • Clean splashback/wall tiles
  • Empty bins
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Clean light switches and door handles
Bathroom
  • Clean and disinfect toilet
  • Clean bath and/or shower
  • Clean shower screen
  • Clean sink and polish taps
  • Wipe mirrors
  • Wipe countertops
  • Wipe towel rail
  • Empty bins
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Clean light switches
Lounge
  • Dust and polish all furniture and surfaces
  • Clean coffee tables and TV stand
  • Wipe mirrors
  • Spot clean glass surfaces
  • Straighten cushions and furnishings
  • Vacuum upholstery (if required)
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Mop hard floors
  • Remove visible cobwebs
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
