Moving In/Out Cleaning

Whether you are settling into a new property or handing one over, this service ensures the space is spotless and ready for its next chapter.

What’s Included in Our Moving In/Out Cleaning Service

  • Full interior cleaning before or after a move
  • Cabinet, appliance, and floor finishing touches
  • Ideal for tenants, landlords, and new homeowners
  • Fast, efficient service for handover deadlines

Normal Cleaning Checklists

Bedroom
  • Wipe wardrobes (outside only)
  • Make beds (if required)
  • Clean mirrors
  • Empty bins
  • Mop hard floors
  • Remove visible cobwebs
Kitchen
  • Wipe and sanitize countertops
  • Clean and polish sink and taps
  • Clean exterior of appliances (microwave, kettle, toaster, fridge, etc.)
  • Wipe hob/stovetop
  • Clean microwave inside and outside
  • Wipe dining table and chairs
  • Clean splashback/wall tiles
  • Empty bins
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Clean light switches and door handles
Bathroom
  • Clean and disinfect toilet
  • Clean bath and/or shower
  • Clean shower screen
  • Clean sink and polish taps
  • Wipe mirrors
  • Wipe countertops
  • Wipe towel rail
  • Empty bins
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Clean light switches
Lounge
  • Dust and polish all furniture and surfaces
  • Clean coffee tables and TV stand
  • Wipe mirrors
  • Spot clean glass surfaces
  • Mop hard floors
  • Remove visible cobwebs
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
